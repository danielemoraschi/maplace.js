# Link to a place

The page's address names the place picked, and the line under the map gives its link: following it opens the map on that place, and the back button steps through what was picked. "View all", the list's first entry, takes it out again.

- **Try it**: on the HTML version, pick Milan: the line under the map gives its link, `?place=milan` on Google and `?place-maplibre=milan` on MapLibre. Follow it, and the page opens again on Milan; press back to step through. This site opens on the HTML version, so only that version's link works here: each version has its own parameter, as a parameter belongs to one map. On your own page, with one version, its link just works.
- **HTML**: `url-state="place"` on `<maplace-map>`, `url-state="place-maplibre"` on MapLibre, does all of it, a place no location has being ignored ([URL state](../../../../docs/reference/element.md#url-state)); `fit-all` and `fit-all-label` add the first entry ([Map attributes](../../../../docs/reference/element.md#map-attributes)). The line under the map is the page's own script, on `maplace-selectionchange`: a link needs only the parameter, and the map's id as its hash so a long page lands on it.
- **Script** and **Bundler**: by hand, in a few lines: `selectedId` from the address, checked against your locations as an unknown id throws ([Options](../../../../docs/reference/store.md#options)); a `selectionChange` listener calling `history.pushState`; and a `popstate` listener calling [`select`](../../../../docs/reference/store.md#methods). `list: { fitAll: true, fitAllLabel }` adds the first entry ([List view options](../../../../docs/reference/store.md#list-view-options)), and the same listener writes the line under the map.
- **React**: `defaultSelectedId` and `onSelectionChange` on `<MaplaceProvider>` ([Controlled selection](../../../../docs/reference/react.md#controlled-selection)), and small components through [`useMaplace()`](../../../../docs/reference/react.md#usemaplace): one selecting on `popstate`, one writing the line under the map.
