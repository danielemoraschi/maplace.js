# Flights

London's flights out, each along its great circle, the shortest way on the earth: a polyline of two points with `geodesic: true`, listed beside the map, while the cities and the plane are on the map only. The flights still scheduled are dashed, and the one to New York is in the air: drawn active, travelled as far as the slider says, with its plane there.

- **HTML**: a `<script type="application/json">` inside `<maplace-map>` holding the data, as a path takes more than an attribute ([Data](../../../../docs/reference/element.md#data)); `list` and `list-heading` on the map ([Map attributes](../../../../docs/reference/element.md#map-attributes)); and a few lines of script for the slider, once the map is ready (`maplace.whenReady()`), setting the flight's `progress` and the plane at `Maplace.pointAlong` through the element's `locations` property ([Properties and methods](../../../../docs/reference/element.md#properties-and-methods)).
- **The looks**: `strokeDash: "dashed"` in a flight's `style` ([Styles](../../../../docs/reference/locations.md#styles)); `active: true` and `progress` on the flight in the air, its travelled part faded unless `travelledStyle` says otherwise ([Polylines](../../../../docs/reference/locations.md#polylines)); the plane a marker at `pointAlong(flight, progress)` ([A point along a line](../../../../docs/reference/locations.md#a-point-along-a-line)), moved with every new set of locations.
- **Script** and **Bundler**: `type: "polyline"`, `path` and `geodesic` on each flight ([Curves and great circles](../../../../docs/reference/locations.md#curves-and-great-circles)), `listed: false` on each city ([Common fields](../../../../docs/reference/locations.md#every-location)), and `list: { container, heading }` ([List view options](../../../../docs/reference/store.md#list-view-options)).
- **React**: the same locations on `<MaplaceProvider>`, and `<LocationList heading="Flights from London" />` beside the `<MaplaceMap>` ([`<LocationList>`](../../../../docs/reference/react.md#locationlist)).
- **On MapLibre, a globe**: `globe` on the map (`globe: true` in the options; in React, `createAdapter={() => createMapLibreAdapter({ globe: true })}`) opens it as the earth, with a "Globe" toggle that flattens it ([Globe](../../../../docs/reference/maplibre.md#globe)). The globe shows London's half: turn it, or flatten it, to see every flight. Google's map has no globe.
