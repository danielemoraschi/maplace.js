# Driving route

A drive through your own places, passing through one without stopping, with its directions leg by leg below the map. On Google, Google computes it live, for any stops you give it. MapLibre has no routing of its own: the page's `computeRoute` answers, and here it replays one route stored beside the example, Milan to Munich (computed once by OSRM; roads © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL), so on MapLibre this route is fixed. In your page, `computeRoute` asks a routing service, and any stops route as on Google.

- **Try it**: pick a place in the list, and the route stays. Switch to MapLibre: the same drive, replayed from the stored route. Read the directions below the map, each leg under a heading with its distance, and the total under them.
- **HTML**: the `route` property, `{ stops, via, travelMode }`, naming your locations by id ([Properties and methods](../../../../docs/reference/element.md#properties-and-methods)), and a `maplace-routechange` listener writing the directions from the result's legs and steps ([Events](../../../../docs/reference/element.md#events)).
- **Script** and **Bundler**: `route` in the map's options, or [`setRoute`](../../../../docs/reference/store.md#methods), and a `routeChange` listener ([Routes](../../../../docs/reference/store.md#routes)); [Routes on Google](../../../../docs/reference/google.md#routes) has its limits.
- **React**: the `route` prop and `onRouteChange` on `<MaplaceProvider>` ([`<MaplaceProvider>`](../../../../docs/reference/react.md#maplaceprovider), [Event props](../../../../docs/reference/react.md#event-props)).
- **On MapLibre**: the same, plus `computeRoute`: the element's property, `createMapLibreMap`'s option or `createMapLibreAdapter`'s; [Routes on MapLibre](../../../../docs/reference/maplibre.md#routes) shows one asking a live OSRM server.
