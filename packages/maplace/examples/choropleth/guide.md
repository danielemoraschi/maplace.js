# Choropleth of the world

The world's countries, each filled by its GDP per person: a country of several polygons, as the United States with Alaska and Hawaii, is one multipolygon location, hovered, selected and listed as one.

- **The data**: `countries.json`, Natural Earth's 1:110m countries, which are public domain, as GeoJSON, each with its GDP per person (its GDP over its population, for years from 2007 to 2019, mostly 2019; none under 10,000 people). Its borders are Natural Earth's, as they stand on the ground; Antarctica is left out.
- **HTML**: `popup-open-on="hover"` on `<maplace-map>`, which opens a country's popup under the pointer ([Map attributes](../../../../docs/reference/element.md#map-attributes)), and a list view of your own, holding the legend and a select of the countries, placed in a corner with `list-position` ([List views](../../../../docs/reference/element.md#list-views)), defined in a plain script so it exists before the map opens; a module script then fetches the countries and assigns `locations`, as the element never fetches.
- **Script** and **Bundler**: [`locationsFromGeoJSON`](../../../../docs/reference/locations.md#from-geojson), each country's `style`, `hoveredStyle` and `selectedStyle` set from its value ([Styles](../../../../docs/reference/locations.md#styles)), `popup: { openOn: "hover" }` ([Popup options](../../../../docs/reference/store.md#popup-options)), and `mountControl` with a [`createListView`](../../../../docs/reference/store.md#list-view-options) select inside the legend.
- **React**: the same locations on `<MaplaceProvider popup>`, and a `<MapControl>` holding the legend and `<LocationList as="select">` ([`<MapControl>`](../../../../docs/reference/react.md#mapcontrol)).
- **On MapLibre, a globe**: `globe` on the map opens it as the earth, with a "Globe" toggle that flattens it ([Globe](../../../../docs/reference/maplibre.md#globe)). Google's map has no globe.
