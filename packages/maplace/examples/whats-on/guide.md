# Your own look

A map that looks nothing like maplace's: a dark frame, pins of your own coloured by category, a count over the map, a legend on it whose chips show one category at a time, and beside it a card of the venue picked. Each piece is one of maplace's hooks; together they are one idea, the whole look the page's own.

- **Try it**: press "Markets", and the map and the count keep the markets; press it again, and every venue is back. Point at a pin, and it lifts; pick one, and it grows, its name beside the count and its address, year and what it is known for in the card beside the map, under it on a phone.
- **HTML**: a `<template data-icon>` in each `<maplace-location>`, with its `group` ([Location attributes](../../../../docs/reference/element.md#location-attributes)); the legend a list view of your own, [`defineListView`](../../../../docs/reference/element.md#list-views), at `list-position="bottom-left"`. The element puts one view of yours on the map, so the count is your own element over the map's top left, where a control would sit, taking no clicks. The card's details are each venue's own `data-` attributes, read by the page's script on the store's `selectionChange` into the card's `<template>`, added to the card while a venue is picked ([Events](../../../../docs/reference/store.md#events)).
- **Script** and **Bundler**: an `icon` node per location ([Icons](../../../../docs/reference/locations.md#icons)), and the count and the legend put on the map with the adapter's [`mountControl`](../../../../docs/reference/store.md#adapters), the count rewritten from [`listedLocations`](../../../../docs/reference/store.md#listed-locations), the name beside it from the snapshot's `selectedId`, the press moved with the store's filter as the store changes, and the card written from the venue's `data` ([Location fields](../../../../docs/reference/locations.md)) on `selectionChange`, its markup a `<template>` in the page; a chip calls [`setFilter`](../../../../docs/reference/store.md#methods) with its category, or with nothing to show them all.
- **React**: `markerIcon` on [`<MaplaceMap>`](../../../../docs/reference/react.md#maplacemap), and the count and the legend in two [`<MapControl>`](../../../../docs/reference/react.md#mapcontrol)s, reading the store with [`useMaplace()`](../../../../docs/reference/react.md#usemaplace), as the card does beside `<MaplaceMap>`, inside `<MaplaceProvider>`.
- **On Google**: its map-type, Street View and camera controls are off (`map-options` in HTML, `mapOptions` elsewhere), for the clean look, as the MapLibre map shows none either.
- **In every version**: every venue fitted with 80px of padding, clear of the count and the legend: the store's `view`, `{ kind: "fit-all", padding: 80 }` ([The first view](../../../../docs/reference/store.md#the-first-view)), or in HTML, which has no attribute for it, [`fitAll`](../../../../docs/reference/store.md#methods) before the map opens. maplace's panel around what goes on the map is made nothing with `.maplace-control { padding: 0; background: none; box-shadow: none; }`, as its rules weigh nothing ([Styling](../../../../docs/reference/styling.md)); each category's colour is one custom property, `--category`, which its pins and its chip take; the pins' hovered and selected looks are rules on `data-hovered` and `data-selected`, as in [Your markers](../css-markers/guide.md). The name of the venue picked is a plain element, not a live region: maplace already says each selection to a screen reader ([On every map](../../../../docs/reference/store.md#on-every-map)). The pins differ by colour alone, so the chips, named, are how a reader picks a category out.
