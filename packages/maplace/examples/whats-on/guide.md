# Your own look

A map that looks nothing like Maplace.js's: a dark frame, pins of your own coloured by category, a count over the map, a legend on it whose chips show one category at a time, and beside it a card listing the venues shown, or the one picked. Each piece is one of Maplace.js's hooks; together they are one idea, the whole look the page's own.

- **Try it**: press "Markets", and the map, the count and the card's list keep the markets, the list named "Markets"; press it again, and every venue is back. Point at a pin, and it lifts; pick one, and it grows, its name beside the count and its address, year and what it is known for in the card beside the map, under it on a phone, in place of the card's list of venues; pick one from the list too, and the card's × brings the list back.
- **HTML**: a `<template data-icon>` in each `<maplace-location>`, with its `group` ([Location attributes](../../../../docs/reference/element.md#location-attributes)); the legend a list view of your own, [`defineListView`](../../../../docs/reference/element.md#list-views), at `list-position="bottom-left"`. The element puts one view of yours on the map, so the count is your own element over the map's top left, where a control would sit, taking no clicks. The card's details are each venue's own `data-` attributes, read by the page's script on the store's `selectionChange` into the card's `<template>`, added to the card while a venue is picked ([Events](../../../../docs/reference/store.md#events)); while none is, the card lists the venues shown, from [`listedLocations`](../../../../docs/reference/store.md#listed-locations), each a button calling [`select`](../../../../docs/reference/store.md#methods).
- **Script** and **Bundler**: an `icon` node per location ([Icons](../../../../docs/reference/locations.md#icons)), and the count and the legend put on the map with the adapter's [`mountControl`](../../../../docs/reference/store.md#adapters), the count rewritten from [`listedLocations`](../../../../docs/reference/store.md#listed-locations), the name beside it from the snapshot's `selectedId`, the press moved with the store's filter as the store changes, and the card written from the venue's `data` ([Location fields](../../../../docs/reference/locations.md)) on `selectionChange`, its markup a `<template>` in the page, and while no venue is, a button per venue shown, rewritten from `listedLocations` when they change; a chip calls [`setFilter`](../../../../docs/reference/store.md#methods) with its category, or with nothing to show them all.
- **React**: `markerIcon` on [`<MaplaceMap>`](../../../../docs/reference/react.md#maplacemap), and the count and the legend in two [`<MapControl>`](../../../../docs/reference/react.md#mapcontrol)s, reading the store with [`useMaplace()`](../../../../docs/reference/react.md#usemaplace), as the card does beside `<MaplaceMap>`, inside `<MaplaceProvider>`, its list read as the ids listed so a hover renders nothing, and `flushSync` rendering it at once so a press can move the focus into it.
- **On Google**: its map-type, Street View and camera controls are off (`map-options` in HTML, `mapOptions` elsewhere), for the clean look, as the MapLibre map shows none either.
- **In every version**: the look is one stylesheet, `whats-on.css`, in the examples' `styles/` folder, which each version's HTML links, so the page, the script, the bundled and the React versions share it, scoped by the frame's class, `.whats-on`, and the map's, `.whats-on-map`. Every venue fitted with 80px of padding, clear of the count and the legend: the store's `view`, `{ kind: "fit-all", padding: 80 }` ([The first view](../../../../docs/reference/store.md#the-first-view)), or in HTML, which has no attribute for it, [`fitAll`](../../../../docs/reference/store.md#methods) before the map opens. Maplace.js's panel around what goes on the map is made nothing with `.maplace-control { padding: 0; background: none; box-shadow: none; }`, as its rules weigh nothing ([Styling](../../../../docs/reference/styling.md)); each category's colour is one custom property, `--category`, which its pins and its chip take; the pins' hovered and selected looks are rules on `data-hovered` and `data-selected`, as in [Your markers](../css-markers/guide.md). A venue picked from the card's list moves the focus to the card's ×, named "Close", which takes it back to that venue in the list, so a keyboard never loses its place. The name of the venue picked is a plain element, not a live region: Maplace.js already says each selection to a screen reader ([On every map](../../../../docs/reference/store.md#on-every-map)). The pins differ by colour alone, so the chips, named, are how a reader picks a category out.
