# A team around the world

Each person is a face on the map, their initials on their team's colour, and the list beside it is the page's own: their name, role and city, and the time where they are next to how far that is from yours, turning as each minute begins.

- **Try it**: find someone by name, city or role, or untick teams in the group filter to see one; pick a face on the map and the list follows. On MapLibre the earth opens as a globe, faces on its far side faded: turn it, or flatten it with the toggle.
- **HTML**: a `<template data-icon>` in each `<maplace-location>` draws the face, and the person's role, city and time zone are the page's own `data-` attributes there, which Maplace.js leaves alone; the template makes the location's `icon` a function ([Templates](../../../../docs/reference/element.md#templates)). The list is a view of the page's own, `Maplace.defineListView` around `Maplace.createListView` with `render`, which reads those attributes from the person's `<maplace-location>` and draws the face with the location's own `icon` ([List views](../../../../docs/reference/element.md#list-views)); `group-filter`, `group-counts` and `search` on the map still reach it as its `options`.
- **Script** and **Bundler**: the people from `team.json`, each given an `icon` that draws the face from its `data` ([Icons](../../../../docs/reference/locations.md#icons)), and `list.render` building each entry ([List view options](../../../../docs/reference/store.md#list-view-options)). The times are written into each entry's own span, and a timeout set to the next minute writes them again, so no location changes for the clock.
- **React**: `markerIcon` on `<MaplaceMap>` and the entry as `<LocationList>`'s children ([`<LocationList>`](../../../../docs/reference/react.md#locationlist)); each entry's `<LocalTime>` keeps the minute in its state, so only the times render again as each minute begins.
- **Styling**: each team's colour, and the rings a face takes while pointed at or picked (`[data-hovered]`, `[data-selected]`), in the examples' `styles/team.css` ([Styling](../../../../docs/reference/styling.md)).
- **On MapLibre, a globe**: `globe` on the map (`globe: true` in the options; in React, `createAdapter={() => createMapLibreAdapter({ globe: true })}`) ([Globe](../../../../docs/reference/maplibre.md#globe)). Google's map has no globe.
